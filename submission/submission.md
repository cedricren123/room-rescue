# Room Rescue submission

Room Rescue finds UCL Science Library group study rooms that fit a student's group size, facilities and schedule. TinyFish Search finds official booking guidance; Fetch reads room details and fresh accessible calendars. The app verifies every interval in the requested session and suggests a later start only when permitted, with links to the evidence.

## Endpoint claim

The shipped app meaningfully uses **Search + Fetch** (two endpoint types). Agent was used to investigate and verify the real booking workflow during development; it is not needed for normal app searches. Do not claim three production endpoint types solely from that development run.

## Demonstration

1. Choose four people, a date within three days, a two-hour session, and allow a start up to one hour later.
2. Click Find our room.
3. Show a matching room, its actual interval and whether it is Plan B.
4. Open its official UCL calendar and compare the session with the available intervals.
5. Increase the group size to show capacity exclusions.
6. Try an invalid date to show an explicit booking-window error.

Saved evidence: `demo.jpg` shows the real app result, and `desktop-live-result.jpg` / `mobile-live-result.jpg` show the full layouts.
